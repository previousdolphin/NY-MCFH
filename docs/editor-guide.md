# Editing nymetrocoalition.org

Go to **nymetrocoalition.org/studio**. Enter your email, then the six-digit code Cloudflare
sends you. If no code arrives, the address is not on the editors list (or has a typo).

## The one idea

Everything you save goes into a **draft**. Nobody sees the draft until you **publish**.
The yellow bar at the top always tells you how many unpublished changes are waiting.

## Add an event

1. **Events**, then **Add event**.
2. Fill in the title, start and end (New York time), venue and description. Choose a poster.
3. **Save to draft**.
4. Press **Review and publish** in the bar at the top. The Publish page walks through three
   steps: review what will change, open the preview, then put it live.
5. Tick "I have checked the preview" and press **Publish now**.

When the event's end time passes it moves to Past Actions by itself. Later you can open it
again to add a short summary and press links.

## Other things you can change

- **Members**: the logo grid under Who We Are. Use the arrows to reorder.
- **Partners**: the cards on the Partner Organizations page.
- **Page text**: headline, mission statement, contact email and so on.
- **Images**: every poster and logo. Add a description to each for screen readers.

In longer text, leave a blank line between paragraphs. `**bold**`, `*italic*` and
`[link text](https://example.org)` work.

## Staging a change

- **Not ready yet?** Untick **Show on site** on the item. It stays in the draft and is left
  out when you publish other things.
- **Want a second opinion?** On **Publish**, press **Copy preview link** and send it. The
  link works for anyone, without signing in.
- **Want it to go out later?** On **Publish**, choose a date and time and press
  **Schedule**. The draft is frozen as it is at that moment. Anything you edit afterwards
  is not included unless you schedule again.

## Undoing

- **Before publishing:** **Discard changes** on the Publish page throws away everything
  unpublished.
- **After publishing:** open **History**, press **Restore…** on an older version, tick the
  box and press **Restore this version**. The live site goes back to it straight away. Your draft is left
  alone, so fix it or discard it before you publish again.

## If something looks wrong

- "Changed by someone else while you were editing": another editor saved the same item.
  Choose **Reload theirs** or **Overwrite with my version**.
- "Your sign-in has expired": use the **Sign in again** link (it opens a new tab), then come
  back and press the button again. Nothing you typed is lost.
